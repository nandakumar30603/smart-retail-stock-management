# 🛒 Smart Retail Stock Management System

> A data-driven approach to improving retail inventory management, stock visibility, demand analysis, and operational efficiency.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Smart Retail Stock Management System** is an academic project focused on improving inventory management in retail environments through modern technologies such as **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, IoT, and real-time data analytics**.

Traditional retail inventory management can face challenges such as stockouts, overstocking, inaccurate inventory tracking, and increased operational costs. This project explores a technology-driven approach to improve stock visibility, support demand forecasting, optimize replenishment, and assist retailers in making data-driven decisions.

This project was developed as part of the **AI Club Project at CMR Engineering College (UGC Autonomous)** during the **2025–2026 academic year**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 📦 Improve inventory and stock tracking
- 📊 Analyze inventory and sales-related data
- 📈 Support demand forecasting
- ⚠️ Reduce stockouts and overstocking
- 🔄 Improve stock replenishment
- 💰 Reduce operational costs
- 👥 Improve product availability and customer experience
- 🚚 Support better supply-chain coordination

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📦 Inventory Management
Improve visibility and management of retail stock levels.

### 🤖 AI & Machine Learning
Apply intelligent and predictive techniques to support inventory-related decision-making.

### 📊 Data Analytics
Analyze inventory and sales trends to generate useful insights.

### 📈 Demand Forecasting
Use historical data and trends to support future stock planning.

### 🔄 Stock Replenishment
Support timely restocking and help maintain appropriate inventory levels.

### 📡 Smart Tracking
Explore the use of IoT, RFID, and barcode-based technologies for inventory tracking.
